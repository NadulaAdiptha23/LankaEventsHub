Our story

One platform for
both sides of the room.

Most event tools serve either the organizer or the guest. Running an event through one and selling seats through another is how a room ends up double-booked and a guest ends up holding a ticket for an evening that moved.

Verdant Events keeps organizers, attendees, events and experiences in one system. The capacity an organizer sets is the capacity a guest sees. The seat a guest books is the seat the door expects. There is no second copy of the truth to disagree with the first.
