What we do

Four disciplines.

01

Event Management

Events are created as drafts, submitted for approval and published deliberately — nothing reaches an audience half-finished. Once people have registered, the terms they signed up to are locked: date, venue, price and capacity stop being editable, because changing them underneath somebody who already booked is not an edit, it is a different event.

02

Event Discovery

Six filters that all reach the database — keyword, category, venue, date range, maximum price and event type, plus “only events with seats left”. Availability shown on a listing is availability that exists, because a seat is held the moment somebody starts booking it.

03

Venue & Logistics

A venue cannot be double-booked: the clash is refused on create and on update, and the check runs against the database rather than against a form. Sessions, schedules and capacity are coordinated in the same place, and capacity can never be cut below the seats already sold.

04

Experience Design

Invitations for restricted events, announcements that reach everyone holding a seat, e-tickets that carry nothing personal, and a door list that records how many of each party actually arrived.

Capabilities

The detail
underneath.

None of this is optional extra. It is what separates an event that runs from one that is survived.

Approval workflow

Organizers submit; administrators approve. Nothing is published by accident.

Held seats

A seat on a paid event is reserved while the attendee pays, and released automatically if they walk away.

Waitlists

Full events keep a queue. When seats come back, the queue moves — automatically, in order.

Party bookings

One booking for up to ten people, counted as ten seats everywhere it matters.

Discounts

Early bird, student, promo code and group rates, applied server-side and never to the wrong event.

Payments & refunds

Card at checkout or cash at the door, with refunds raised automatically when an event is cancelled.

Door check-in

Record who arrived — and how many of each party — from a phone at the entrance.

Reporting

Attendance, revenue, refunds and category performance, computed live and exportable as CSV.
